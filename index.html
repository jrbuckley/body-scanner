    Pick an existing JPG to run the same ratio analysis on it. No camera needed. On a phone this picker opens your photo library.

    How it works: line yourself up until every check turns green, hold still — the countdown starts on its own. Front first, then turn 90° for the side shot (either direction). The moment a shot is captured, the pose model runs on the still right here and the ratios appear under Analysis — deltas compare against your station baseline once it's been set from your first station session. Downloads still land as progress-YYYY-MM-DD-front.jpg / -side.jpg if you want the files.

    Protocol that keeps the ratios honest: same spot and distance, arms relaxed at your sides, similar shorts, camera at about waist height.
